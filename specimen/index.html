Pollen

By Nidheesh Vakharia

Pollen is a free, open-source typeface with two dials: weight, from Regular to Bold, and roundness, from crisp corners to soft ones. It grows out of Open Runde, the rounded take on Inter, which only came as four separate weights. Pollen joins them into one variable file, fills in every weight in between, and adds roundness, so a single font can go from sharp to soft.

Article

How a meadow keeps its own diary

Every spring, a fine yellow layer settles on ponds and lakes. Most of it sinks, and the record it leaves can last for thousands of years.

That layer is pollen. Each year it is buried by mud and more pollen, and over centuries the thin layers stack up into an archive of the plants that grew nearby.

Reading the layers

Every species makes a grain with its own shape. Count the grains in each layer, and you can tell what the landscape looked like long ago.

“The mud doesn’t forget. It just waits for someone to ask the right question.”

From grain to forecast

Pollen does more than record the past. The same grains that settle in lakes also drift through city air every spring, and counting them tells people with hay fever what kind of day to expect.

Traps on rooftops pull in a steady stream of air and catch whatever floats past on a sticky strip. Each morning someone counts the grains under a microscope and sorts them by plant.

Birch tends to peak first, then oak and grasses follow as the weather warms. A dry, windy week can carry grains for hundreds of kilometres before they come down.

Rain does the opposite. It washes the air clean, which is why the hour after a shower is often the easiest time of the day to be outside.

      About Pollen

      • What is Pollen?

        Pollen is a free, open-source variable typeface by Nidheesh Vakharia. One font file covers weight 400–700 and roundness 0–100, from sharp corners to soft ones.

      • Is Pollen free for commercial use?

        Yes. Pollen is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, so you can use it in personal and commercial work, including websites and apps. You can't sell the font files on their own, and copies must keep the copyright notices and the license.

      • What is Pollen based on?

        Pollen is built from Open Runde by Laurids Kern, a rounded take on Inter by Rasmus Andersson. Pollen joins Open Runde's four static weights into one variable font and adds a roundness axis.

      • What does the roundness axis do?

        The ROND axis runs from 0 to 100. At 100, the default, corners are fully rounded like Open Runde. At 0 they are sharp, close to Inter. Values in between blend the two.

      • How do I use Pollen on a website?

        Download Pollen[ROND,wght].woff2, load it with @font-face and font-weight: 400 700, then set font-weight for weight and font-variation-settings: "ROND" 0–100 for roundness.

      • Which languages does Pollen support?

        Pollen covers Latin (including accented letters), Greek and Cyrillic, with 2,494 characters.
