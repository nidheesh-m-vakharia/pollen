Text

Open Runde is a soft, rounded take on Inter. It shipped as four static weights; this fork stitches them back together into one variable font with a single weight axis, so the space between Regular and Bold is no longer empty.

Each master was flattened on its own, so node counts drifted between weights. The build aligns nodes along every contour by arc length and splits segments where one weight has a point the others lack — the masters' shapes are reproduced exactly, and the in-between weights interpolate smoothly.

Notes

Kerning and mark positioning come from Regular at every weight for now. 15 rare glyphs whose contour topology changes between weights (e.g. Ⓦ, ₦, ₩, Ҋ) stay at their Regular shape.
